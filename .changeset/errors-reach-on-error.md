What an invariant, the collection of new or observed options, a part's selector or the adapter's painting throws reaches `onError` like a step's error: outside strict mode the widget keeps its previous state, drops the actions of a commit that failed, `render` keeps the attributes it rendered last, and the adapter still paints the other elements. `ConnectionEnvironment.onError` takes the action as optional.
