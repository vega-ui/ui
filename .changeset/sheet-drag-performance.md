Overlay parts of `Dialog`, `Drawer`, `Popover`, `Select`, `Sheet` and `Tooltip` no longer detach and attach a passed `ref` on every render. Make `Sheet` drag run without React renders on its moves, layout or style recalculation of its content. `--sheet-offset`, `--sheet-expansion` and `--sheet-clip` are no longer inherited by the children of `SheetContent`. `blurred` on `SheetContent` now defaults to `false`, like a native iOS sheet. The surface of the sheet is now a separate layer: set its color with `--sheet-background` instead of `background`. `::after` of `SheetContent` is now taken. The content of `SheetContent` keeps its width between snap points, with `--sheet-inset` added to its side padding. The sheet follows the pointer exactly, stops under a press while it settles, and with `steppedSnapPoints` resists a drag past the next snap point. It settles on a curve without overshoot, and stops below the top safe area. A light flick no longer closes the sheet: momentum counts less towards closing than towards a snap point. The sheet closes over the same duration as it opens, and after a drag it continues at the speed of the pointer. Snap points that share an offset report the last index, and a drag back to the active offset keeps the active snap point
