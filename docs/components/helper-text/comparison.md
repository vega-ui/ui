# HelperText Comparison

## Quick Decision Rule

Use `HelperText` for short supporting copy near a field. Use `Text` for general copy and `Label` for the field name itself.

## `HelperText` vs `Text`

Use `HelperText` when:

- the copy is field-adjacent guidance

Use `Text` when:

- the copy is ordinary body content

## `HelperText` vs `Label`

Use `HelperText` when:

- the copy is supplemental guidance

Use `Label` when:

- the text is the primary visible field label

## `HelperText` vs validation banners

Use `HelperText` when:

- the guidance or error is local to one field or one grouped control

Use a larger alert or summary pattern when:

- the error is form-wide or cross-field

## Choose This Component When

- copy belongs directly to one control or control group
- the tone should stay secondary or validation-adjacent
- the content should remain visually subordinate to the label and input

## Do Not Choose This Component When

- the copy is the main label
- the content is long-form explanation
