# MonthPicker Comparison

## Quick Decision Rule

Use `MonthPicker` when users should choose a month from a grid. Use `DayPicker` for specific dates and `YearPicker` for year selection.

## `MonthPicker` vs `DayPicker`

- Use `MonthPicker` for month-level choices.
- Use `DayPicker` for day-level selection.

## `MonthPicker` vs `YearPicker`

- Use `MonthPicker` for the month within a year context.
- Use `YearPicker` when the year itself is the target.

## Choose This Component When

- the value is a month
- a dense month grid is the right interaction

## Do Not Choose This Component When

- users need a specific date or year instead of a month
